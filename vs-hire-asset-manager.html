Where the human still wins

If your portfolio has significant owner-relationship complexity — venture-backed PM with weekly investor calls, 100+ owners with varying communication preferences, regulatory disputes requiring strategic judgment — you still need a human. Vesara handles the analytics + recommendations + execution. A human handles relationships + judgment + escalations. The right answer at scale is usually both — Vesara plus 1 human at half a $85K hire's salary doing only the relationship work.

The hybrid model (what we recommend)

For PMs with 25-100 properties, the optimal staffing is:

  • Vesara Pro ($599/mo) for the analytics + recommendations + monitoring across all properties
  • 1 part-time owner-relations specialist (~$35K/yr) for owner conversations, conflict resolution, in-market presence
  • You stay strategic — focus on portfolio growth + new owner acquisition, not daily pricing decisions

Combined cost: $42K/yr vs. $98K/yr for a single full-time asset manager who also can't be in two places. Net savings: $56K/yr, with better coverage.
