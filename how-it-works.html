● How it works

Connect once. Decide better, every day.

Vesara reads your data through a one-time OAuth, runs every recommendation through three independent AI models in parallel, and surfaces only what's worth your time. No new system to learn. No manual data entry. No black-box AI.

Step 1
Connect
One-click OAuth to your PMS (Guesty / Hostaway / OwnerRez). 10 minutes total. No API key paste.
Step 2
Score
22-dimension property scoring per listing. Triple-brain consensus computes the 0-100 grade.
Step 3
Recommend
Top 5 opportunities ranked by impact × confidence. Sourced reasoning, not opaque AI.
Step 4
Apply
One click pushes back to your PMS or PriceLabs. Or modify, or skip. You stay in control.
Step 5
Measure
Vesara Lift Score attributes specific revenue lift to specific decisions. Closed-loop ROI.

Triple-brain consensus

Three AI models. One decision.

Single-AI is overconfident. We run every high-stakes recommendation through three independent large language models in parallel — ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google), Claude (Anthropic). Only when at least 2 of 3 agree does the recommendation surface to you. When they disagree, that's often more useful than agreement — it tells you the decision is genuinely uncertain.

What Vesara does NOT do

Anti-features (by design).

These are intentional product decisions. Vesara is not trying to be everything — we're trying to be the AI Asset Manager. The list below is what we will never build, no matter how often customers ask:

  • Replace your PMS. Guesty/Hostaway/OwnerRez are good at being PMSes. We'd be a worse one.
  • Process bookings. Your channel manager already does this.
  • Handle guest messaging. Your PMS handles ops messaging. We handle strategy.
  • Sell guest data to third parties. Ever. Privacy is contractual, not preferential.
  • Auto-push pricing without your approval. Default is recommend, not act. Autopilot tier is opt-in.
  • Provide legal, medical, or licensed financial advice. We surface data; licensed advisors interpret it.
  • Build a hardware product. Smart locks, noise sensors — others do this well, we integrate with them.
  • Become a marketplace for properties. We're tooling, not a transaction platform.
