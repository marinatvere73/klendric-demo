Brand · v1.0

The Vesara mark.

A chamfered hexagon — the geometry of infrastructure — wrapped around an aperture: an inverted triangle that reads as lens, action, and downward arrow. Built to sit on the same shelf as Stripe, Linear, and Vercel.

The System

Six variations. One geometry.

Every asset uses the same chamfered hexagon and inverted triangle. Color, fill, and lockup change for context — never the geometry itself.

Sub-Product Family

One geometry. Six products.

Each Vesara product wears the same chamfered hexagon. The inner symbol changes — Score speaks numbers, Lens sees, Studio creates, Capital secures, Marketplace connects, OS commands. The tint shifts to give each product its own voice without breaking the family.

Color

One accent. One ground.

Clear Space

Give the mark room.

Minimum clear space on all four sides equals the cap-height of the wordmark (the "x" measure). Never crowd it with adjacent text, logos, or borders.

Don't

Six things that break the mark.

The mark survives at 16px. The rules are short. Follow them.

Don't
No gradients on the mark.
Don't
Don't change stroke weight.
Don't
Don't rotate the mark.
Don't
Don't stretch or distort.
Don't
Don't recolor outside the system.
Don't
Don't add hard shadows or 3D.
