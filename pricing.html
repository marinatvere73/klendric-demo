Common questions

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Your account auto-converts to the tier you signed up for. Cancel anytime before day 14 and you're charged nothing. We never charge a card without sending a reminder first.

Is the Founders' Flock pricing really locked forever?

Yes. $99/month, never increases, for the lifetime of your subscription. Even when we go public, even when we add features, even when standard tier hits $599. Founders' Flock is permanent.

Can I change tiers later?

Anytime. Upgrade is instant + prorated. Downgrade takes effect at the next billing cycle. Founders' Flock customers can upgrade to Pro or Autopilot at the standard difference (e.g., $99 Founders + $200 to upgrade to Pro = $299/mo total, with the $99 portion locked).

What if I have 100+ properties?

Enterprise tier is custom. White-glove CSM, dedicated infrastructure, custom integrations, SLA guarantees. Contact marina@vesara.ai directly.

Is my data safe?

SOC 2 Type II audit in progress. All data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Field-level encryption for PII. You can request data deletion anytime — completed within 30 days. We never sell or share your operator or owner data with anyone.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, ACH (US), SEPA (EU), BACS (UK). Powered by Stripe.

Do you have a refund policy?

14-day free trial means you don't risk anything to start. After conversion, we don't issue automatic refunds — but if something goes wrong and Vesara doesn't deliver value, talk to Marina directly. We've never lost a customer who genuinely needed help.
