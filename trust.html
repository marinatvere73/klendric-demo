The 5 principles

Promises, not platitudes.

●
You own your data — always.
Property, owner, guest, financial — all yours. We process it to provide the service. We don't claim ownership. Self-serve export at any time.
●
We never sell your data.
Not to OTAs. Not to insurers. Not to marketing platforms. Not at any price. Contractual, not preferential.
●
Right to delete, completed in 30 days.
Self-serve in Settings. Full deletion within 30 days. Some financial records retained per US tax law (7 years). No retention hostage-taking.
●
Comp Pool privacy by design.
k-anonymity ≥5 + differential privacy (epsilon = 1.0). No addresses pooled. No owner/guest/financial detail pooled. One-click opt-out.
●
72-hour breach notification.
If a breach affects you, you're notified within 72 hours of our discovery — including what was affected, what we did about it, and what you should do.
