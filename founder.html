01.The breaking point

I started LussoStay in 2021 with two properties in Reno. By 2024, I had 28 properties across Reno, Sparks, and San Diego. The whole journey was supposed to be about operational excellence — luxury vacation rentals run with the precision of a hotel chain, but with the design sense of a boutique.

Instead, by year three, I was spending 40% of my week on three things:

  1. Trying to figure out why a specific property was underperforming
  2. Explaining pricing decisions to owners who were politely skeptical
  3. Manually pulling data from 6 different tools to make one decision

I was paying for PriceLabs (excellent — but only does pricing). I was paying for AirDNA (excellent — but it's read-only data, not decisions). I was paying for Guesty (excellent — but it's a database, not a brain). I was running a spreadsheet on the side that was the "real" source of truth, and that spreadsheet was held together by my Sunday afternoons.

"There's no AI Asset Manager for vacation rentals. Every tool tells you what happened. Nothing tells you what to do."

That sentence — said out loud to a friend in late 2024 — is when Vesara started.

02.What I went looking for

I wanted one thing: a tool that would look at all my data — pricing, listings, reviews, owner relationships, comp set, channel mix — and tell me "this is the next thing you should do, and here's why." Not a dashboard. Not a report. A decision.

I evaluated 14 tools. Demos with most of them. Free trials with eight. Bought four of them outright over the course of 18 months. None of them did it. They all stopped at "here's the data, you figure out what it means."

That's when I understood: nobody is going to build this for me. Not because it's not buildable, but because the people who can build it don't operate properties, and the people who operate properties can't build it.

03.What Vesara is

Vesara is the AI Asset Manager I wanted to buy. It sits on top of your existing PMS — Guesty, Hostaway, OwnerRez, doesn't matter. It reads your pricing data from PriceLabs. It pulls comps. It scores every listing on 22 dimensions. It runs every recommendation through three independent AI models (ChatGPT + Gemini + Claude) and shows you where they agree, where they disagree, and what the consensus is.

Then it tells you: here's the next thing to do. Here's why. Here's the dollar impact. Apply, modify, or ignore.

That's it. That's the entire product. Everything else — the owner portal, the comp pool, the voice agent, the autopilot tier — exists in service of that one promise: turn data into decisions, faster than your competition can.
