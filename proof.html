▶ The 5 audit principles (binding on every claim)

Why this isn't marketing.

●
Every rec is logged before implementation
Recommendation, confidence score, predicted impact — committed to git before the change goes live. Lock-in beats hindsight bias.
●
Every outcome is measured against a counterfactual
A "lift" claim requires showing what would have happened without the rec. Trailing-30-day baseline per property. No baseline = no claim.
●
Misses get the same airtime as wins
Honest counter-data section in every monthly stakeholder pack. If a Vesara rec backfired, it gets published. Trust is built on the misses, not the wins.
●
Recommendation accuracy is computed quarterly
Aggregate `actual / predicted` across all measured recs. Target ≥75% by Q3 2026. Below 60% means we adjust the triple-brain prompts. Above 90% means we're sandbagging predictions.
●
All artifacts are version-controlled
Audit logs, weekly reports, monthly packs, QBRs — committed to a public git repo. Stakeholders can verify nothing was retroactively edited.
▶ The cadence we operate on

Daily, weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly.

Daily
15 min · Marina + AI Coach
8:00 AM PT
Vesara dashboard auto-refreshes overnight. AI Coach surfaces top 5 recs ranked by impact × confidence. Marina triages: AUTO-APPROVE / REVIEW / DEFER. Auto-approved recs push immediately via PriceLabs/Guesty MCP. Marina time: 5-10 min.
Weekly
30 min · Monday 9 AM PT
Generated each Monday
Lift Report computes week-over-week trajectory, top 3 wins + top 3 misses, accuracy by category. Course-corrections logged. Posted to Founders' Flock Slack when public.
Biweekly
60 min · Every other Friday
Owner variance + portfolio sweep
Owner-facing variance memos drafted. Cross-portfolio audit pattern (like the SD3902 fix) re-run on all 27 properties. Vesara product backlog reviewed.
Monthly
90 min · Last Monday
Stakeholder Proof Pack
4-tier distribution: Investors get full pack. PM owners get their property-specific extract. Founders' Flock cohort gets anonymized aggregate. LussoStay team gets ops review.
Quarterly
1 day · End of quarter
QBR + product roadmap
Full Quarterly Business Review with cumulative Lift, recommendation accuracy, NRR impact, owner satisfaction lift, product features shipped from pattern-mining log, Founders' Flock vote results.
