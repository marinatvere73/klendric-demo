▶ The owner conversation that ends contracts

The 4 moments that lose you the property.

Every PM has had this conversation. The owner emails: "I'm thinking about going direct." Or worse, they don't email — they just sign with someone else and you find out at renewal time. These are the four moments that trigger that decision.

01
"Why was my March payout lower than February?"
Owner sees a $487 dip and assumes you're skimming, slacking, or making bad decisions. You have to dig through Guesty + PriceLabs + spreadsheets to construct the answer. By the time you respond, they've already drafted the breakup email in their head.
02
"Why did you lower my rates?"
PriceLabs dropped the rate on a soft Tuesday. Made operational sense. Looks like you devalued their property. You explain dynamic pricing for the fifth time. They say "got it" but file it under "concerns about the manager's pricing discipline."
03
"My friend's property is doing better than mine."
Their friend's property in a worse location is somehow earning more. You know it's because their friend has 5 more bedrooms and a hot tub — but defending the comp set with no data feels weak. Owner walks away believing the manager is the problem.
04
Annual review with no proof of value
Once a year you sit down to renew. You have a verbal "we did a great job this year" — they have 12 months of selective memory. The conversation is anchored on whatever happened last week. You lose the contract you saved $200K worth of revenue on.
