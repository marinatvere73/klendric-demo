The problem

Operators have data, not decisions.

Vacation rental operators run businesses with $1M-$25M annual gross. They use 4-8 specialized tools — Guesty for ops, PriceLabs for pricing, AirDNA for data, Breezeway for cleaning, QBO for accounting. Each tool is good at one thing.

None of them are an asset manager. Operators spend 40% of their week stitching data together to make decisions. Owners get poor reporting. Pricing is reactive. Listings drift. There's no decision layer.

03 / 12
The solution

Vesara is the brain on top of the stack.

We sit on top of existing PMS (Guesty, Hostaway, OwnerRez), pricing tools (PriceLabs, Beyond), and data tools (AirDNA, Key Data). We aggregate the data the operator has scattered across these tools and turn it into prioritized decisions.

Triple-brain consensus (ChatGPT + Gemini + Claude in parallel) on every recommendation. Property scoring across 22 dimensions. Anonymous comp pool with k-anonymity ≥5. Owner Portal that owners actually use. Closed-loop ROI attribution via Vesara Lift Score.

The product: "Here's the next decision worth making, here's why, here's the dollar impact, apply or modify."
