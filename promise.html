The Founders' Flock Lifetime Promise.

Effective: 2026-05-17 · Locked-forever clause is contractual, not marketing copy.

This document exists because words on a pricing page are easy to write and easier to forget. The Founders' Flock promise — first 100 customers pay $99/month for the lifetime of their subscription — is the foundational commitment of Vesara, Inc. It is binding. It does not expire. It is not subject to "circumstances changed" revisions or "we evolved our pricing strategy" emails sent at 5pm on a Friday before a holiday weekend. It is real.

Below is exactly what we promise, exactly what could change, and exactly what will not. Read it twice before you sign up. We mean every word.

The core promise: If you become one of the first 100 paying Vesara customers, your monthly subscription price is $99 USD per month, forever. We cannot raise it. We cannot deprecate it. We cannot move you to a different tier. Your $99/mo seat persists for as long as you choose to remain a Vesara customer.

01.What "lifetime" means

Lifetime means the lifetime of your active Vesara subscription, not your biological lifetime. If you cancel and resubscribe later, the new subscription is at then-current pricing — your Founders' Flock seat does not reactivate. As long as your card on file is current and your subscription is in good standing, the $99/mo lock is permanent.

Specifically:

  • Pricing changes for new customers never affect you. When standard tier moves to $599/mo, you stay at $99/mo.
  • New product tiers never affect you. When we launch Vesara Enterprise at $5,000/mo, you stay at $99/mo on Founders' Flock.
  • Acquisition or merger never affects you. If Vesara is acquired, the acquirer inherits this promise as a binding obligation.
  • Bankruptcy is the only thing that ends this promise. If Vesara ceases to exist as a legal entity, no contract can survive that.

02.What features you get — also locked

Founders' Flock includes everything in the Standard tier at signup, plus the Founders'-only benefits listed on the pricing page (permanent badge, Slack/SMS line to Marina, retreat invitation, roadmap voting). When we ship new Standard-tier features, you get them automatically — your $99/mo includes the full Standard product roadmap forever.

Tiers above Standard (Pro, Autopilot, Enterprise) are not included. If you want to upgrade, you pay the difference between Founders' Flock and the upgrade tier — for example, Pro at $599/mo would cost you $99 (locked) + $500 (the upgrade differential at then-current Pro pricing). The locked $99 portion never changes.

03.What we explicitly will not do

  • We will not raise your price citing "inflation," "cost increases," "rising compute costs," or any other reason.
  • We will not deprecate the Founders' Flock tier or migrate you to a "successor" tier.
  • We will not introduce new mandatory add-ons that effectively raise your bill (e.g., "now $99/mo plus a $50/mo platform fee").
  • We will not gate critical features behind paywalls that did not exist when you signed up.
  • We will not sell your operator data, your owners' data, or your guests' data to third parties at any price.
  • We will not change this promise via a Terms of Service update buried in fine print.

04.What might change (and how we'll handle it)

Stripe processing fees are paid by Vesara out of the $99, not added to your bill. If Stripe doubles their fees, we eat the cost.

Compute costs for AI inference (the triple-brain consensus, Vesara Score generation) are paid by Vesara out of the $99. If OpenAI/Anthropic/Google raise their API prices 10x, we eat the cost.

Property count limits on Founders' Flock are 25 properties. If you grow past 25, you can either stay capped (we'll only score 25 of them) or upgrade to a tier that supports more — your $99/mo lock persists either way.

05.How this is enforced

This promise is incorporated by reference into the Vesara Subscription Agreement (Section 4.3, "Founders' Flock Lock"). Any change to this document requires (a) written consent from a majority of then-active Founders' Flock subscribers, or (b) a court order. Vesara's Board of Directors does not have unilateral authority to amend this promise. Marina Tinova, as CEO and majority shareholder, has personally signed below to bind the company.

If we ever appear to be violating this promise — through pricing emails, billing changes, or feature deprecation — email marina@vesara.ai directly. She personally answers Founders' Flock issues within 24 hours and will resolve any dispute in your favor by default.

06.Why this exists

Most SaaS companies raise prices on their earliest customers as soon as they have leverage. We chose not to. The first 100 people who took a chance on Vesara when we were unproven get permanent recognition for that risk — financially, in the badge, in the direct line to leadership, in the roadmap voting rights. This is not marketing. It is the founding promise of the company, and the day we break it is the day Vesara stops being worth running.
