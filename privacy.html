Privacy Policy

Short version: your data belongs to you. We don't sell it. We don't share it with OTAs or competitors. You can export everything at any time. Delete it on request.

●You own your data. Property records, owner records, guest records, financial records — all yours. We process it to provide the service. We don't claim ownership.

●You may opt out of the Comp Pool. Aggregate benchmarking uses k-anonymity ≥5 and differential privacy. Opt-out is one click in Settings.

●SOC-2 Type II. Audited annually. Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).

02.Who we share with (a short list)

We share data with the following categories of service providers, under contracts that prohibit them from using your data for any purpose other than serving you:

  • Stripe (payments) — billing data only
  • Supabase (database hosting) — encrypted at rest
  • Vercel (web hosting) — your account info and product data when you use the app
  • OpenAI / Anthropic / Google (LLM providers for triple-brain) — prompts only, with zero-retention agreements where available
  • Postmark (transactional email) — recipient email + message body
  • Sentry (error monitoring) — stack traces, scrubbed of PII
  • Cloudflare (CDN + DDoS) — request metadata

We do not share with: advertising networks, data brokers, OTAs, competitors, marketing platforms, or any party that would use your data to market to you, your owners, or your guests.

03.The Comp Pool — opt-in by default for Pro+, opt-out anytime

Vesara's Comp Pool aggregates pricing and performance data across operators to power benchmarking. Specifically:

  • Every aggregate query requires k-anonymity ≥5 (your data only contributes if 4+ other operators in the same cohort also opted in)
  • Differential privacy (epsilon = 1.0) noise is added to every output
  • Property addresses are never included in pooled data — only generalized geography (city or sub-market level)
  • Owner identity, guest identity, and financial details are never pooled

Opt out anytime: Settings → Privacy → "Don't include my data in Comp Pool." No service degradation; you just lose access to comp benchmarks for properties similar to yours.

04.Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you have these rights:

  • Access — see all data we hold on you. Self-serve via Settings → Data Export.
  • Correction — fix incorrect data. Self-serve in product or email privacy@vesara.ai.
  • Deletion — request full deletion. We complete within 30 days. Some financial records retained per tax law.
  • Portability — export in machine-readable CSV/JSON.
  • Objection — opt out of any processing where we use legitimate-interest as a basis.
  • Complaint — file with your local data protection authority (if EU/UK).

05.EU + UK residents (GDPR)

For EU/UK customers, Vesara is the data controller. Our EU representative is a fractional appointment via OneTrust (contact via privacy@vesara.ai). Data is stored in US data centers by default; Frankfurt residency available on Pro+ tier under a separate Data Processing Agreement.

Legal bases for processing: (a) contract performance for service delivery; (b) legitimate interest for product improvement, security, and fraud prevention; (c) consent for the Comp Pool (Pro+ tier opt-in default with one-click opt-out); (d) legal obligation for tax and accounting records.

06.California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

Vesara does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You have the right to know what we collect, to delete, to correct, and to limit use of sensitive personal information. To exercise: privacy@vesara.ai or Settings → Privacy.
